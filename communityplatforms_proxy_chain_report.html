Platform Referrals
Revenue Proxy Chain Analysis

Jan 2025 – May 2026  ·  n = 17 months  ·  Spearman rank correlation · Plausible data · Community platforms

Abstract & Method

This analysis tests whether the proxy chain platform activity → referral traffic → website exploration → free trial clicks holds for community platforms. The goal is twofold: to describe what the data shows about how community platform activity connects to site outcomes, and to identify which metrics can credibly represent this contribution in the absence of direct input-level tracking.

Platforms covered: Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, GrowthHackers, Substack, Slack — analysed both in aggregate and individually. Website exploration is measured via unique visitors, pageviews, views per visit, and bounce rate. Free trial clicks are the closest available downstream revenue proxy.

Method: Spearman rank correlation throughout, chosen for robustness with small, real-world datasets. Same-month and 1-month lag tested. Directional agreement (% of consecutive months both variables moved the same direction) used as an N-independent cross-check. With n=17, results are informed hypotheses, not conclusions.

What the signals mean

The chain holds through step 3

Platform → referrals → site exploration: r = +0.77

The link from community platform referral traffic to unique visitors is strong (r = +0.77, p < 0.001). When referral activity across platforms goes up, site visitors go up the same month.

The pageviews lag (r = +0.61 at T+1) also holds — suggesting some referral traffic leads to return visits or deferred exploration the following month.

Recommended proxies Track monthly community platform referral visitors (primary) and total pageviews (secondary) as your measurable contribution to site exploration.

Free trial clicks: chain breaks here

Non-YT refs vs FTC: r = −0.00 · UV vs FTC: r = +0.14

There is no detectable link between community platform referral traffic and free trial clicks. This is the honest limit of the current data.

Two likely explanations: (1) community platform referral volume is still too low to generate consistent conversions — most months see 0–2 FTC events total; (2) n=17 with so few non-zero FTC months is simply too small to detect a real but weak relationship.

This does not mean no relationship exists — it means the data cannot confirm one yet.

Honest framing Use referral traffic + site exploration as your proxy chain. Free trial clicks remain aspirational — revisit at n=24.

Reddit is the reliable channel signal

r = +0.64 vs UV · p = 0.006 · 69% directional agreement

Reddit is the only individual channel with a statistically reliable relationship to site visitors (p = 0.006) and consistent directional agreement (69%). It accounts for 63% of community platform referral volume.

When Reddit activity is up, the site is up. This makes Reddit referral visitors a viable individual-channel proxy metric.

Action Reddit referral visitors are worth tracking as a standalone metric alongside the aggregate.

Referral quality: bounce rate signal

Community platform refs vs bounce rate: r = −0.50 · p = 0.042

Higher community platform referral months correlate with lower bounce rates (r = −0.50). This suggests platform-referred visitors are not just arriving — they're exploring.

High-referral months (May–Jun 2025, Apr–May 2026) have bounce rates of 8–17%. Low-referral months (Jan–Mar 2025) run at 33–48%. The quality argument is real.

Action Add bounce rate as a quality proxy alongside volume. A falling bounce rate in a high-referral month strengthens the contribution argument.

Proxy chain assessment

Platform activity → referral visitors → website exploration → free trial clicks

What the data supports — and what it honest about.
  • The measurable contribution is real. Community platform activity produces referral visitors, and those visitors correlate strongly with site UV (r = +0.77) and pageviews (r = +0.66). This is not a claimed input-to-output story — it is an observed output-to-outcome relationship that holds consistently across 17 months.
  • Step 4 does not hold yet. There is no detectable link between community platform referral traffic and free trial clicks at n=17. FTC events are too sparse (most months: 0–1) to establish correlation. This is a data-density problem, not necessarily a real-world absence of effect — and it is stated as such.
  • Reddit is the strongest individual signal. The only community platform channel with a statistically significant independent relationship to site visitors (p = 0.006). Instagram is borderline significant (p = 0.046). TikTok's correlation is a timing artifact, not a channel effect.
  • Bounce rate strengthens the contribution case. High-referral months bring more engaged visitors (bounce rate 8–17% vs 33–48% in low-referral months). This adds a quality dimension to the volume argument.
  • Recommended tracking set: Monthly community platform referral visitors (primary), Reddit referral visitors (channel-level), monthly pageviews (depth, holds at T+1 lag), and bounce rate (quality check). Re-run at n=22 (Oct 2026) when Substack and Slack will have 10 months of comparable data.
