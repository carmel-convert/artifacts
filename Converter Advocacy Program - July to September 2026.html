Engagement rate held in August (3.2% vs 3.0%). Posting more raised Ahmed's total reach, but each post reached fewer people. This is the main trade-off to watch in the next window.

06Reading notes & open items

  • Directional only. Three participants, one three-month window, no control group. These are signals, not proof.
  • Counted from submissions; measured on LinkedIn only. Counts come from the publish log, which is the source of truth. Metrics come from the LinkedIn exports. Medium and Substack stats aren't included. Rosh's Reddit post shows engagements only.
  • Metrics are summed across exports. Each post's figures add up its July, August and September exports, so September posts have had less time to build up numbers than July posts. The May–Jun rates on each bucket use the same method: v1's figures plus whatever those posts gained in the Jul–Sep exports.
