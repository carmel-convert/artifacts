The Reddit instalment (Scattered notes, May 9) sits in this series but has no available metrics.

The siloing cost, quantified. The CRO long-form lives only on Medium, where it drew ~19 views in its launch month — while the short LinkedIn post pointing to it drew 1,075 impressions. Impressions and views aren't the same unit, but the funnel leak is real: the audience is on LinkedIn, the article is on Medium. Meanwhile Docs-AI, kept on LinkedIn, reaches further (2,224 impressions) but engages at less than half CRO's rate (1.3% vs 3.2%) — so the stronger-engaging content is the one that's hidden.
