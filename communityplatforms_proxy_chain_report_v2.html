Revenue Proxy Chain Analysis

Jan 2025 – Sep 2026  ·  n = 21 months (v1: n = 17)  ·  Spearman rank correlation · Plausible data · Community platforms

Abstract & Method

This analysis tests whether the proxy chain platform activity → referral traffic → website exploration → free trial clicks holds for community platforms. The goal is twofold: to describe how community platform activity connects to site outcomes, and to identify which metrics can credibly represent that contribution without direct input-level tracking.

Platforms covered: Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, GrowthHackers, Substack and Slack, analysed in aggregate and individually. Website exploration is measured with community-only bounce rate and visit duration (Plausible segment "Distribution Optimizer (CM) – Only"). Free trial clicks are the closest available downstream revenue proxy.

Data: Jan 2025 – Sep 2026 (n = 21 months; v1 used Jan 2025 – May 2026, n = 17).

Method: Spearman rank correlation throughout, chosen for robustness with small, real-world datasets. Same month and 1-month lag tested. Substack and Slack are tested on 2026 only (n = 9). A year-on-year comparison uses Jan–Sep of each year. With n = 21, results are informed hypotheses, not conclusions.

Data notes

  1. Free trial clicks: an average of 0.25/month for Jun–Sep 2026, vs an average of 0.58/month over the previous 12 months. Plausible only credits a source when the Free Trial button is clicked in the same visit.

What the signals mean

Volume more than doubled

Jan–Sep: 164 → 362 community visitors · Reddit 49% · Instagram 28%

Community platforms sent more than twice as many visitors to the site year on year (Jan–Sep: 164 → 362). Of the four steps in the chain, this is the strongest result. Instagram (+147%) and Slack + Substack (82 visitors, not tracked in 2025) drove most of the growth.

Action When reporting the impact of community work, lead with the number of visitors community platforms send to the site each month.

Bounce holds while volume grows

31% → 32% · r = −0.33 vs volume (not significant)

Doubling traffic often raises bounce. It didn't here: community bounce stayed around a third, and leaned slightly lower in high-volume months.

Action Track community bounce alongside volume. Flat or falling bounce in a high-volume month supports the contribution case.

Visits got shorter

Median 89s → 36s · r = −0.51 vs volume · p = 0.018

The only significant website exploration result (step 3 of the chain), and it's negative: high-volume months bring shorter visits. Months with only 5–9 community visitors can have a high average from one or two long visits (e.g. Apr 2025: 3m 8s). Busier months include more quick visits, which pulls the average down (e.g. Jun 2026: 29 visitors, 6s).

Action Check which pages community links land on. Landing on a resource with a clear next step is the main lever for visit depth.

Free trial clicks: still breaks

vs volume r = −0.03 · vs bounce r = −0.14 · vs duration r = −0.11

No exploration metric links to free trial clicks, and FTCs fell (8 → 2, Jan–Sep) while volume doubled.

Plausible only credits the source if the trial button is clicked in the same visit, so shorter visits make that less likely.

Action Claim volume and bounce. Treat free trial clicks as not yet attributable to community.

What changed from v1, and what it means.
  • Step 2 (referral traffic) holds and is growing: community visitors +121% year on year (Jan–Sep).
  • Step 3 (website exploration) is mixed: bounce is stable as volume doubles, but visits are shorter.
  • Step 4 (free trial clicks) still breaks.
  • Recommended tracking set: community referral visitors (primary), Reddit + Instagram visitors (channel level), community bounce (quality check), community visit duration (depth, watch the trend).
